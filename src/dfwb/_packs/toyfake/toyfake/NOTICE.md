## Access

generated locally with `dfwb datasets synth toyfake`; there is nothing to download

Licence: MIT, with dfwb: synthetic videos generated from a seed, with no real person in them

## What these files hold

Never media: this folder lists video keys, labels, split assignments and fake/real pairs, derived from the release's file names and metadata. It holds no videos, frames, crops, face boxes, landmarks or anything else derived from pixels.
